[STEAM_TITLE:Custom DFHack Menu]
[STEAM_DESCRIPTION:Requires DFHack. Make the DFHack logo menu yours. Add any DFHack command - with or without a hotkey - remove the ones you never use, put them in your own order, give them your own names and group them under headings. Assign a hotkey to any command just by pressing it. Pin favourites to a toolbar of buttons under the top information bar - it grows to fit, wraps when needed, lights up commands that are switched on, and a small button next to the DFHack logo shows or hides it. Add commands straight from the DFHack launcher with Ctrl-K. It starts with the same list DFHack shows, so nothing changes until you edit it - press Ctrl-E in the menu. No need to activate it in your mod list - subscribing is enough. Unsubscribe and the normal DFHack menu comes straight back.]
